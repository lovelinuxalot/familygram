Child safety standards

Familygram · last updated 2026-08-30

Familygram is a private, invite-only photo app for families. These are our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Zero tolerance

Familygram prohibits child sexual abuse material and any content or conduct that sexualises, exploits, or endangers a child. This applies to photos, captions, comments, usernames, and display names. There are no exceptions, and the prohibition applies equally to every family using the app.

How the app limits risk by design

  • No public access. There is no public sign-up, no public profile, no search, and no discovery feed. Nothing in Familygram is reachable by a member of the public or indexed by search engines.
  • Invitation only. An administrator must add a person's email address before that person can sign in at all. Sign-in is via Google or Apple.
  • Closed groups. Each family is a separate, isolated space. Members of one family cannot see the photos, comments, or members of another.
  • No strangers, no messaging. There is no way for an unknown adult to contact a child through Familygram: there is no direct messaging, no friend requests, and no way to reach anyone outside your own invited family.
  • Private media. Every photo is served through a short-lived signed link. There is no permanent public URL for any image.

How to report a child safety concern

In the app. Tap the ⋮ menu on any post or comment that isn't yours and choose Report. Pick Child safety concern — it is the first option — add any detail, and send. The report reaches the administrator immediately.

By email. You can also write to {{CONTACT_LINK}} with "CHILD SAFETY" in the subject line. Include which family the content is in, who posted it, and roughly when. You do not need an account to send an email report.

Members can also raise a concern directly with their family's administrator, who can remove any post or comment and revoke a member's access immediately.

How reports are handled

  • Reports are reviewed on receipt and prioritised above all other correspondence.
  • Content assessed as CSAE is removed immediately, and the responsible account's access is revoked.
  • Confirmed CSAM is reported to the relevant authority — in the United States, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and to national or regional authorities elsewhere as the law requires.
  • Evidence is preserved as required by law before deletion, and is not returned to the reported account.

Compliance

Familygram complies with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where it operates, including obligations to report child sexual abuse material to the competent national authorities. We comply with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and its CSAE prohibitions.
