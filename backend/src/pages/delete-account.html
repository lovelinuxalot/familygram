Delete your Familygram account

Last updated 2026-08-30

This page explains how to delete your Familygram account and what happens to your data. Familygram is a private, invite-only family photo app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Familygram app and sign in.
  2. Tap You in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately — there is no waiting period and no separate request to submit. You are signed out as soon as it completes.

If you can no longer sign in

If you've lost access to your account and cannot reach the button above, ask the admin of your family — whoever invited you — to delete the account on your behalf. Each family runs its own private Familygram instance, so that admin is the data controller for your account.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account record — email address, username, display nameDeleted immediately
Photos you uploaded, and their thumbnailsDeleted from storage immediately
Your profile pictureDeleted from storage immediately
Your posts and their captionsDeleted immediately
Comments and likes you wroteDeleted immediately
Comments and likes other people left on your postsDeleted immediately, together with your posts
Your family membershipsDeleted immediately
Push notification tokens for your devicesDeleted immediately
Your invitation recordDeleted immediately

What is kept

DataRetention
Your sign-in identity held by our authentication provider, Ory Network — the email address linked to your Google or Apple sign-inNot removed by the in-app deletion. To erase it, ask your family admin to delete your identity in Ory, or contact them to request it on your behalf.
Infrastructure logs held by our hosting providers (Cloudflare, Ory)Retained by those providers under their own retention schedules, typically a short rolling window. These are operational logs, not app content.

Nothing else is retained. Familygram collects no analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or location data, so there is no additional profile to delete.
